# We cut our support reply time from 9 hours to 2

Last spring our small support team of 4 people was answering customers in about 9 hours on average. Customers told us that felt slow, and 1 in 5 tickets was reopened because the first answer missed the point.

We made three changes over 6 weeks.

First, we sorted every new ticket into one of 5 topics before a person read it. Billing questions went to the person who knows billing, instead of to whoever was free.

Second, we wrote 30 saved replies for the questions we see every week, such as password resets and invoice copies. Each saved reply is a starting point that the agent edits, not a canned answer.

Third, we stopped closing tickets automatically after 3 days. A ticket now stays open until the customer says the problem is solved.

The result: our average first reply time dropped from 9 hours to 2 hours, and reopened tickets fell from 20% to 8%. The team did not grow, and nobody works longer hours.

If you run a small support team, start with sorting. It took us one afternoon to set up and it made the other two changes possible.
